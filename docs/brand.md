# Gouvra — brand

**Name.** From French *gouverner* (to steer a ship), Latin *gubernare*, Greek *kybernân* —
"to pilot". The same root gives English *govern*: Gouvra steers agents and governs them
(permissions, budgets, quotas). Pronounced "goo-vra".

**Mark.** A "G" drawn as a steering dial: the open arc is the dial, the bar is the heading
needle, the light dot is the pivot. Rounded square tile, blue gradient `#3B7BFF → #1D4ED8`,
white strokes (3/32 of the tile), pivot `#9BE7FF`. Source: `web/src/components/brand/logo.tsx`,
favicon: `web/public/favicon.svg`.

**Wordmark.** "gouvra", lowercase, Inter SemiBold, letter-spacing −0.02em.

**Concept.** "Control room for AI agents": calm surfaces, data first, colour reserved for
actions and states. Motion serves information — states, arrivals, feedback — never ornament.

**Illustrations.** Line drawings built from design tokens (they follow light/dark themes),
lightly animated (floating nodes, flowing links), still under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
Source: `web/src/components/brand/illustrations.tsx`.
