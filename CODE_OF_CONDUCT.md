# Code of Conduct

We adopt the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1. In short:

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment: empathy and kindness, respect
for differing opinions and experiences, giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback,
taking responsibility for our mistakes and learning from them, focusing on what is best for the
community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes: sexualised language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention;
trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks; public or private
harassment; publishing others' private information without their explicit permission; any other
conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers clarify and enforce these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, code, issues and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.
Report incidents privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting on the
repository; all reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the reporter is
respected. Consequences follow the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: correction,
warning, temporary ban and permanent ban.
